Support

Sidewalk Assess, by American Sidewalks LLC

Need help or found a problem? Email americansidewalks.com@gmail.com. We usually answer within one business day.

How do I report a sidewalk problem?

Open the app, tap Report a Sidewalk Issue, take a photo, tap what you see (crack, raised edge, curb ramp, and so on), then tap Upload.

My report says Waiting. Is that a problem?

No. Waiting means your phone had no connection. The report is saved and uploads by itself when you are back online. Uploading means it is sending now, and Sent means we have it.

Why does the app need my location?

So your town knows exactly which sidewalk you mean. Location is used only while the app is open. If you turned it off, go to Settings, then Sidewalk Assess, then Location, and choose While Using the App.

The camera is black.

Go to Settings, then Sidewalk Assess, and turn Camera on.

How do I delete a report?

Swipe left on it in My Reports and tap Delete. That removes it from your phone. To remove a report you already sent from our records, email us the report number (it starts with SW-).

Who fixes the sidewalk?

Your town, city, or county decides which repairs to make. Your report puts the problem on its list with a photo, an exact location, and a priority score.

I work for a town. How do we get our reports?

American Sidewalks provides each town a private repair spreadsheet and map built from resident reports, with photos, GPS pins, and a daily crew list. Email us to start a pilot.
