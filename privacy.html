Privacy Policy

Sidewalk Assess, by American Sidewalks LLC, Citrus Springs, Florida. Effective November 23, 2026.

Sidewalk Assess is a free app that lets you photograph a sidewalk problem and send it to the town where it is located. This policy explains, in plain English, what the app collects and what we do with it.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email, or phone number in the app.
  • We collect only what is in a report you choose to send: the photo, its location, and the details you tap.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your information.

What a report contains

When you tap Upload, the app sends:

  • The photo or photos you took of the sidewalk.
  • The GPS location where you took it, plus the street and town name your phone looks up from that location.
  • The defect tags you picked (for example, "Trip hazard" or "Curb ramp"), a priority score the app calculates, and any note you typed.
  • The date and time, the app version, and a report number. The report number includes a short code made from a random ID the app creates when it is installed. That ID is not tied to your name, your Apple ID, or your phone's advertising ID.

Please avoid photographing people's faces, license plates, or the inside of anyone's home. If one appears by accident, we may blur or remove it.

Camera and location

The app uses the camera only when you take a report photo. It uses your location only while the app is open, to place the report on the right street. It does not track your location in the background. You can turn off either permission at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

Where reports go and who sees them

  • Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored in American Sidewalks LLC's Google Workspace (Google Sheets and Google Drive). Google stores the data for us under its own privacy and security terms.
  • American Sidewalks staff review reports to prioritize sidewalk repairs.
  • We share reports with the town, city, or county where the sidewalk is located, so it can plan repairs. Once a government agency receives a report, it may be subject to that agency's public records laws (for example, Florida's public records law).
  • We may publish summary information, such as the number of hazards found on a street, without any information that identifies you.
  • We do not sell or rent reports or any personal information, and we do not use them for advertising.

Information on your phone

Your reports and photos are also saved on your phone so you can see them in My Reports and on the map, and so they can upload later if you have no signal. Deleting a report in the app removes it from your phone. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone.

How long we keep reports

We keep reports as long as they are useful for planning and tracking sidewalk repairs, usually until the repair is finished plus a few years for records. You can ask us to delete a report at any time (see below).

Your choices

To ask us to delete a report you sent, or to ask any question about your information, email americansidewalks.com@gmail.com with the report number shown in the app (it starts with SW-). We will respond within 30 days.

Children

Sidewalk Assess is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
